

\# Elastic AlertZero - Risks and Human Oversight



\## Incorrect AI Decisions



AI workers may make incorrect security decisions.

They may identify normal activity as a security threat

or fail to detect an actual cyberattack.



For example, an AI worker might identify an employee's

unusual login activity as a security threat even though

the employee is simply working from another location.



\## Overdependence on AI



Cybersecurity teams may become too dependent on AI.

Security analysts might accept AI recommendations

without properly checking the information.



Human expertise is still important because AI

can make mistakes.



\## Data Privacy Concerns



AI workers may need access to sensitive information,

including security logs and employee login details.



Organizations should carefully control what information

AI systems can access and share.



\## Importance of Human Supervision



Human supervision helps prevent incorrect security

decisions and unnecessary actions.



According to Gemini's research, AlertZero provides

different levels of autonomy, allowing security teams

to control how independently AI workers operate.

This should be checked against Elastic's documentation.



\## Conclusion



AI workers can help cybersecurity teams reduce their

workload and improve security investigations.

However, human supervision remains important to

ensure responsible and reliable decision-making.



These are potential risks of AI in cybersecurity,

not confirmed incidents involving AlertZero.



\## Discussion Question



Should companies allow AI to respond to cybersecurity

threats automatically, or should human approval

always be required?
