# Elastic AlertZero - Working and Benefits

## How Does AlertZero Work?

AlertZero uses specialized AI workers to help cybersecurity teams investigate security alerts and identify potential threats.

These workers perform repetitive tasks in the background so security analysts can focus on more important decisions.

## Four Main Areas of Operation

AlertZero organizes its AI workers into four groups called Watches.

### 1. Triage Watch

Reviews incoming security alerts and groups related suspicious activities together.

### 2. Hunt Watch

Searches system data for potential security threats that existing detection rules may have missed.

### 3. Detection Watch

Reviews existing security rules and recommends improvements to reduce false alarms.

### 4. Forensics Watch

Investigates security incidents, collects evidence and recommends possible response actions.

## Automation and Human Control

AlertZero workers can operate at different levels of independence, depending on their settings.

In Manual mode, proposed actions require human approval. In Supervised mode, selected actions can be performed automatically.

Changes to detection rules always require human approval.

## Potential Benefits

•⁠  ⁠Reduces repetitive manual work.
•⁠  ⁠Helps security analysts investigate threats faster.
•⁠  ⁠Reduces the workload of cybersecurity teams.
•⁠  ⁠Helps analysts focus on important security threats.
•⁠  ⁠May reduce employee fatigue caused by excessive alerts.

## Conclusion

AlertZero aims to improve cybersecurity operations by combining AI automation with human expertise.

However, these potential benefits depend on the accuracy of the AI system and how it is configured.
