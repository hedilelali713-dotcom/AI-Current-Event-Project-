# Elastic AlertZero - Background and Introduction

## What is AlertZero?

AlertZero is an AI-powered cybersecurity technology developed by Elastic. It uses specialized AI workers to assist cybersecurity teams with investigating security alerts and identifying potential threats.

## When Was AlertZero Introduced?

Elastic announced AlertZero in October 2026. It became available as a technical preview in November 2026 as part of Elastic Security 9.6.

## What is a Security Operations Center (SOC)?

A Security Operations Center is a team responsible for monitoring and protecting an organization's computer systems, networks and data from cyberattacks.

## Why Do Cybersecurity Teams Need AI?

Cybersecurity teams receive large numbers of security alerts every day. Many of these alerts may be false alarms.

Manually investigating these alerts can be time-consuming and overwhelming for security analysts.

AlertZero aims to help analysts by collecting information, organizing security alerts and assisting with investigations.

## Purpose of AlertZero

The main purpose of AlertZero is to reduce repetitive work and help cybersecurity professionals focus on important security threats.

However, its potential benefits depend on the accuracy of the AI system and proper human supervision.
