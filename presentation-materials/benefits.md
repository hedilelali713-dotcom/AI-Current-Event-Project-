# Presentation Script - Working and Benefits

AlertZero organizes its AI workers into four main groups called Watches.

The Triage Watch reviews incoming security alerts. The Hunt Watch searches for potential threats. The Detection Watch helps improve security rules, while the Endpoint Forensics Watch investigates security incidents and collects evidence.

These AI workers can perform repetitive tasks in the background, allowing cybersecurity professionals to focus on more important investigations.

The potential benefits include reducing manual work, speeding up investigations and helping security teams manage large numbers of alerts.

However, these benefits depend on how accurately the AI works and how it is configured.

Although this technology offers several advantages, there are also some important risks to consider.
