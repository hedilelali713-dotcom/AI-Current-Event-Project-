# Presentation Script - Introduction

Hello everyone. Today, we are going to talk about Elastic AlertZero, a recent development that combines artificial intelligence with cybersecurity.

Elastic announced AlertZero in October 2026 and introduced its technical preview in November 2026.

A Security Operations Center, also known as a SOC, is a team responsible for monitoring computer systems and protecting organizations from cyberattacks.

These teams receive large numbers of security alerts, and many of them are false alarms. Investigating every alert manually can be time-consuming.

AlertZero aims to solve this problem by using AI workers to assist cybersecurity professionals.
