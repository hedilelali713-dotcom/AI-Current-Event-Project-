\# Presentation Script - Risks and Conclusion



Although AI workers can help cybersecurity teams, they also introduce potential risks.



For example, AI may incorrectly identify normal activity as a security threat or fail to recognize an actual cyberattack.



Another concern is that security teams may become too dependent on AI. There are also privacy concerns because AI systems may need access to sensitive information.



Human supervision is important because it helps prevent incorrect decisions and unnecessary security actions.



In conclusion, AlertZero shows how AI could improve cybersecurity operations, but automation should be balanced with human responsibility.



Our discussion question is: Should companies allow AI to respond to cybersecurity threats automatically, or should human approval always be required?
