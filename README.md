\## Project Overview



This project explores Elastic AlertZero, a recent

development in artificial intelligence and cybersecurity.



AlertZero uses specialized AI workers to assist

Security Operations Centers (SOC) with security

alerts, threat detection and investigations.



Our project examines how AlertZero works, its

potential benefits, risks and the importance

of human supervision.



\## Key Research Findings



\- AlertZero uses four groups of AI workers called Watches.

\- AI workers can reduce repetitive manual tasks.

\- AI can help security teams investigate threats.

\- AI may produce incorrect decisions or miss threats.

\- Human supervision and data privacy are important.
