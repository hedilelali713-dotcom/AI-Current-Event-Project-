\# Partner 3 - Reflection



\## A3. Comparison - What Was Similar? What Was Different?



Both AI models appreciated our choice of topic,

the explanation of AlertZero and our discussion

of the benefits and risks of AI.



Both models suggested completing the AI evaluations

and reflection document.



However, their suggested grades were different.



| Category | ChatGPT | Gemini |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Content and Presentation | 19/25 | 22/25 |

| GitHub Collaboration | 21/25 | 5/25 |

| AI Evaluation Setup | 8/25 | 10/25 |

| Reflection Quality | 5/25 | 10/25 |

| Total | 53/100 | 47/100 |



ChatGPT reported that it could access our GitHub

repository but could not properly review our video.



Gemini reported reviewing our video but could

not access our GitHub repository.



ChatGPT provided more useful feedback about

GitHub collaboration, while Gemini provided

more specific feedback about our video.



\## A4. Do You Agree With the AI's Grade?



We partially agree with the suggested grades.



Both AI models correctly identified that our

evaluation and reflection files were unfinished

at the time of evaluation.



However, we do not completely agree with the

GitHub collaboration score given by Gemini.



Our group used branches, commits, Issues,

Pull Requests and Discussions throughout

the project.



Gemini could not access this work, which

affected its suggested grade.



Similarly, ChatGPT could not properly verify

our video and deducted marks for that reason.



We believe both grades were affected by

the limitations of the AI models rather

than the quality of our work alone.



\## A5. What Would You Do Differently?



If we completed this project again, we would

provide more supporting evidence to both AI

models during the evaluation.



For example, we could provide screenshots of

our GitHub commits, Pull Requests and Issues

if an AI could not access our repository.



We would also provide the presentation script

if an AI could not review our video.



We would check the video duration more carefully

and verify all research sources before submission.



We would not change our research topic or

discussion question simply because the AI

models gave us lower grades.



This experience taught us that AI feedback

can be useful, but it should not replace

human judgment.
