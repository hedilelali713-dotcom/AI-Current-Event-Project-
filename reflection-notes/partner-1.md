## B1. How You Used AI During Research

We used Google Gemini and ChatGPT during our project.

Gemini helped us understand Elastic AlertZero,
its purpose and its role in cybersecurity.

ChatGPT helped us organize our research, prepare
presentation scripts, create slides and understand
the GitHub workflow.

We also used research articles and official
documentation to support our information instead
of relying entirely on AI-generated answers.

## B2. What Worked Well

AI helped us understand technical information
in simpler language.

For example, we asked Gemini to explain the
background and purpose of Elastic AlertZero.

It helped us understand how AI workers can assist
Security Operations Centers with security alerts
and investigations.

ChatGPT also helped us organize our research into
presentation sections, making it easier for each
team member to understand their responsibilities.

This saved time and helped us organize our project.
