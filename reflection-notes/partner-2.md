# Partner 2 - Reflection

## A2. AI Model 2 - Summary

We used Google Gemini as our second AI model
to evaluate our project.

Gemini provided the following scores:

- Content and Presentation: 22/25
- GitHub Collaboration: 5/25
- AI Evaluation Setup: 10/25
- Reflection Quality: 10/25
- Overall Score: 47/100

Gemini appreciated our choice of a recent AI topic,
the structured explanation of the four Watches
and our final discussion question.

It suggested improving GitHub documentation,
completing both AI evaluations and writing
a detailed reflection.

Gemini reported that it could review our YouTube
video but could not access our GitHub repository.

It also reported that our video was 3 minutes
and 7 seconds long, which was slightly above
the required time limit.

## B3. What Did Not Work Well

AI was not always able to access all the
information needed to evaluate our project.

For example, Gemini could not access our GitHub
repository and gave us only 5 out of 25 for
GitHub collaboration, even though our group
had completed many GitHub tasks.

ChatGPT could access parts of our repository
but could not properly verify our video.

During presentation preparation, we also noticed
that ChatGPT introduced additional research
references. We needed to check these references
and make sure they supported our topic.

These experiences showed us that AI-generated
information should not be accepted without
checking its accuracy.

## B4. Overall Reflection

Using AI helped our group understand technical
concepts, organize tasks and prepare our
presentation more efficiently.

However, we learned that AI has limitations
and cannot always access or evaluate all
the available information.

If we completed a similar project again,
we would continue using AI for research
assistance and organization, but we would
verify its answers more carefully.

The most important lesson we learned is that
AI is a helpful tool, but human judgment
and critical thinking are still necessary.
